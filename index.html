Common questions

How do I connect with a family member?

Open the app and go to Connections, then choose to scan a QR code or show your own. Point your camera at the other person's code to send an invite. Alternatively click on "tips" link on your profile screen

I'm not receiving invites or updates

Please confirm with your relative if they can see the invite record in their app under connection/family members. They must click on the envelope icon.

How do I sign in with Apple or change my sign-in method?

You can manage connected sign-in methods from Settings within the app. Click the sign-out button and you will be shown a login screen. If you've forgotten your login password, click on the 'Forgot Password" link.

How do I delete my account or data?

Click on the "Delete" button at the bottom of your profile screen and follow the prompts. If you run into trouble, email us and we'll complete the deletion for you.
